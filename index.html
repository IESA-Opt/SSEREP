Scenario-space exploration for energy-system optimisation models

Interactive companion to the paper. Every figure below is the one printed in the article, redrawn from the same numbers so it can be zoomed, hovered and read off directly.

Ensemble of 10,000 Latin hypercube evaluations and 3,200 Morris evaluations over 31 uncertain inputs, solved with IESA-Opt at 3-hourly resolution for the Netherlands in 2050.

Data behind every panel is in results/; the model is in model/. Drag to zoom, double-click to reset, hover for values.
